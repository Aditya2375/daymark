What is measured

Split-adjusted one-minute bars are grouped into 78 five-minute returns. The first starts at the 09:30 open; later returns run from one five-minute close to the next. Overnight returns are excluded.

Each half-hour's sum of squared log returns is divided by the session total. This is a realized-variance share, not annualized volatility. Volume uses shares traded in that half-hour divided by all observed regular-session share volume. Multiply either share by 13 to get the chart's index.

What the clock leaves out

Only days with all 390 regular-session minute bars are kept. No gaps are filled. Pre-market, after-hours and bars stamped 16:00 or later are excluded. Any auction activity assigned to a 16:00 bar is outside this window. Ten complete days remain for each asset; none were dropped in this sample.

Per-day normalization removes the level of daily activity. The shaded range is the smallest to largest observed daily share, not uncertainty about a population mean. News and a single large move can dominate a bin.

A study, not a rule

Ten recent sessions and two liquid assets do not establish a stable seasonal law. There is no fitted U-curve, hypothesis test, forecast or trading signal here. Five-minute sampling and the treatment of the open affect the result.
