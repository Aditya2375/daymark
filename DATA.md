# Data provenance and reuse

## Chosen source

[FirstRate Data free samples](https://firstratedata.com/free-intraday-data), downloaded 15 November 2026. Two free archives, no account or purchase:

- https://x01.frd-data.com/firstratedata_sample_SPY.zip
- https://x01.frd-data.com/firstratedata_sample_AAPL.zip

These URLs are generated by the provider's published sample-page template, not a private endpoint. Each archive contains multiple bar sizes. Only `SPY_1min.csv` and `AAPL_1min.csv` are used privately. The actually downloaded files cover **2 to 13 November 2026**, despite the sample-page catalogue describing an older September window. The data itself is the source of the displayed dates. The archives rotate; hashes and member names are frozen in [data/provenance.json](data/provenance.json).

The sample-page specification calls these split-adjusted bars, timestamped at bar start in US Eastern (EST/EDT), with volume in shares traded. Extended hours are present. Each asset has 3,900 regular-session bars across ten complete weekdays. SPY has 5,404 outside-session bars; AAPL has 4,953. No incomplete core session was dropped. None of those raw bars is in this repository or deployment.

## Permission and public boundary

FirstRate Data's [license](https://firstratedata.com/about/license) grants Subscribers the right to create derivative works and describes public charts, visualizations and research results as permitted, while restricting raw-data redistribution. It defines a Subscriber as anyone who downloads data through its website, and 'the Data' as financial-market data provided by FirstRate Data. The agreement does not separately name free samples; this project's reading that the free samples are covered rests on those definitions. Its [FAQ](https://firstratedata.com/about/faq) also describes derived statistics and rendered results as permitted, but raw-bar CSV/API/table distribution as not permitted.

The public `web/data/shares.csv` is original derived analysis: dimensionless within-day half-hour shares of realized variance and observed regular-session volume. It contains no original prices, raw bar volume, absolute volume totals or minute bars. There is no raw-data export feature. Code and these original derived outputs are MIT OR Apache-2.0. The underlying vendor data is not relicensed, supplied or made open by this project.

Attribution: Derived analysis from FirstRate Data free historical samples. FirstRate Data does not endorse the analysis. See [NOTICE](NOTICE). No proprietary source archive should ever be committed, included in screenshots, or served alongside the demo.

## Reproduction

Compile the Java aggregator, retain the two exact source CSVs privately, then run:

```sh
python3 tools/rebuild-data.py /private/SPY_1min.csv /private/AAPL_1min.csv
python3 tools/verify-data.py
node tools/build-web.mjs
```

The script verifies input hashes and the expected output hash before writing. It emits only normalized derived results. Newer rotating samples will fail that fingerprint check rather than silently changing the archive. Raw inputs are deliberately not bundled, so a complete raw-to-derived rebuild requires the exact privately retained inputs. Public CI verifies the frozen result fingerprint and tests the entire aggregator with synthetic bars, without fetching vendor data.
